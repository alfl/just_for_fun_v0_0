## Future Directions

Notes from frabrication, observing viewer interactions, and key critical
comments:

- The breathing makes the hiding unclear. One thing at a time.
- Perhaps need better affordances to give "permission" to trespass.
- Perhaps two sensors straddling the painting element would be more functional
  than a single sensor under it.
- The LEDs were wrong. White LEDs non-functional during fabrication, swapped in
  reds.
- The interaction of stepping on the clear styrene was unanticipated.
- Generally, this should have been completed earlier so there was more time to
  process the semantics of the piece.
- Neopixel animation is perhaps too harsh.
- There are deep personal resonances in this piece that will not make sense to
  other people: the crushed material sandwich from Science Venture, the
  mirroring of my studio painting practice, the joy of recycling studio
  materials.
- Other pieces in the show typically revealed themselves to the viewer. This one
  (uniquely?) hid from the viewer.
- The foam pads allowed grip, but more grip was needed, and generally more
  hardening against viewer interaction.
- Viewer interactions caused the LEDs to migrate.
- Perhaps hide the transition to the piece, eg with a rug, rather than direct
  "footprint" affordances. I like the idea of requiring trespass without
  permission, and perhaps without initial intention.
- The diffusion hiding the power cable was not fully aesthetically integrated.
- Generally, there is a conversation amongst the materials that needed more.
- If it's a painting that fell off the wall, it's actually upside-down and
  backwards, and the wall could have been painted or decorated indicatively.
- I was thinking about Walter Benjamin's idea of the non-filmic nature of
  painting. Could have done more with this with more time.
- Why is the frame broken. Why is the base broken. What is the violence? More of
  the conversation implied by the materials. All of the pieces have a voice. Are
  they talking to each other? What was framed?
- Q: Why the material of the digikey wrapping? A: Interesting diffusion, but one
  viewer commented that "cardboard leaves" perhaps imply Autumn.
- Is it a bed? Is it a grave? Dimensions confusing. Ghost vibes.
- Is it that the digital doesn’t like the painting?
- Is it about living in an art gallery?
- The intention of the colony living under glass didn’t come through clearly.
- Error and failure and violence and the broken. Painting. Sensory violence.
  Reuse of the broken.
- The approach is a violent act. The entities hide from the approach, and the
  gaze of the viewer as their curiosity draws them into further transgression.
- "It’s a wall that’s on the floor." Maybe a door. Maybe a window.
- "It's a portal."
- "It has a dark energy."
